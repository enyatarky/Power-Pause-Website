Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to the most common questions about Power Pause and how the card game helps office workers stay active during the day.

Power Pause is a movement-based office card game that encourages office workers to take short movement breaks throughout the workday.

Simply shuffle the deck, draw a movement card and complete the exercise. The activities are designed to be quick, fun and suitable for the workplace.

Most Power Pause activities take between one and five minutes, making them easy to fit into a busy workday.

Power Pause is designed for office workers who spend long periods sitting and want a simple way to include more movement in their day.
