Office workers often sit for long periods of time, which can lead to back and neck pain, bad posture, eye strain, mental fatigue and lower energy levels.

Power Pause turns passive office breaks into short movement moments using playful, office-friendly cards that can be done alone or with colleagues.

  1. Pick a card from the deck.
  2. Complete the movement for around 30 seconds.
  3. Collect points and return to work refreshed.
