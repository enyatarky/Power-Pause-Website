Take better movement breaks at work

Power Pause is a movement-based office card game that helps office workers take short, structured and playful movement breaks during the workday.

Try the break recommendation tool

The problem

Many office workers sit for long periods and skip movement breaks. This can make the workday feel less active, less structured and less energising.

The solution

Power Pause gives office workers simple movement cards that can be used during short breaks. The goal is to make movement more accessible, playful and easy to start.

How it works

  1. Pick a Power Pause card.
  2. Follow the short movement instruction.
  3. Take a quick break and return to work refreshed.
